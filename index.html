Interactive Stadium Map

How to Use the Map

Find your section, food, restrooms, gates and more across every level of Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

Touch controls

Drag to move

Slide one finger to move around the map.

Pinch to zoom

Spread two fingers to zoom in, pinch to zoom out.

Twist to rotate

Turn two fingers to spin the stadium.

Two-finger slide to tilt

Slide two fingers up or down to see the stadium in 3D.

Tap a spot

Tap any location to see its name, details and options.

Find what you need

  1. Search

    Tap the search bar and type a section number, concession, restroom or gate.

    Section 223Gate 1RestroomsTeam Store

  2. Browse by category

    Not sure what you want? Tap a category to see every option at once, like food, drinks or restrooms.

  3. Switch levels

    Use the level picker to move between floors. Locations on other levels stay hidden until you switch.

    Field100S1200S2300

  4. Open the location card

    Tap a result to center the map on it and pull up its details. Swipe the card up for more or down to see the map.

  5. Get directions

    Tap Directions, choose your starting point and follow the highlighted route, even across levels.

Mobile tips

Scrolling the page?Swipe outside the map. Inside the map, your finger moves the map instead.
Lost your bearings?Zoom out to see the full stadium, then search again.
Need an accessible route?Turn on the accessible option in directions to avoid stairs and escalators.
Still have a question?Ask Arthur, our chatbot, for help with parking, policies and more.

Mouse & trackpad controls

Click & drag to move

Hold the left mouse button and drag to move around.

Scroll to zoom

Use the scroll wheel, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom in and out.

Right-click & drag to rotate

Drag left or right to spin. Drag up or down to tilt into 3D.
